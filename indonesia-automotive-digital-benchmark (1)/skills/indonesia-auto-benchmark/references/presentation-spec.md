# Executive presentation specification

Create an editable 16:9 PowerPoint in the user's language and visual style when provided. Default to a restrained, high-contrast design with consistent brand labels and legible screenshots. Give each slide one takeaway; keep claims tied to evidence.

Suggested sequence (adapt to scope):

1. Title, brands, Indonesian market and research date.
2. Executive summary: 3–5 evidence-backed findings and implications.
3. Scope and method: domains, surfaces, definitions, scoring, coverage and access limitations.
4. Journey map or category profiles showing where capabilities appear.
5. Capability matrix across brands, split across slides if needed for readability; include status legend and maturity scale.
6. Common, developing, advanced and distinctive features, with sample denominator.
7. Two to four feature deep dives, each with a real screenshot, short observation, URL and capture date. Annotate only what is visible.
8. Opportunity priorities with rationale, uncertainty and dependencies.
9. Appendix: evidence register with direct URLs, checked dates, scores, and unresolved questions.

Use screenshots from official Indonesian pages or label other surfaces explicitly. Crop without altering the substance; ensure text is readable. Do not create simulated website screenshots or imply that a static screenshot proves completion of a workflow. If a screenshot is unavailable, show the URL and observed evidence in a simple text panel and supply a capture list specifying the exact page and interface state for manual capture. Keep all charts and matrices editable where practical. Verify that hyperlinks work, sources remain on the relevant slides, and nothing clips or becomes illegible in rendered slide previews.
