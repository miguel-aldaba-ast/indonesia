# Scoring and evidence protocol

## Status and maturity

| Status | Meaning | Required treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confirmed | Direct official Indonesian evidence demonstrates the capability | Link the exact page and describe observed behavior |
| Partial | Entry point exists, but the full action, local availability or integration is unverified | Explain the missing step; do not award full maturity |
| Not found | Consistent inspection of relevant official pages and search paths found no evidence | Record pages and search paths checked; avoid categorical “absent” |
| Unknown | Access blocked, broken, ambiguous, or not inspected | Do not score as zero |

Score maturity only for confirmed or partially evidenced capabilities: 0 = not found after documented inspection; 1 = informational or outbound contact without a demonstrated functional result; 2 = interactive tool with observed output or structured request with a submission step; 3 = observed transfer of the customer-specific result into a subsequent step (finance, dealer, account, order or equivalent); 4 = demonstrably completed outcome with confirmation, tracking or cross-channel continuity. The number of fields, detail of a finance breakdown, or a claimed integration alone does not raise a score to 3. Use N/A for irrelevant capabilities and ? for unknown. A score of 0 must retain its “not found” qualifier. A promotional claim alone supports at most informational maturity.

## Evidence register

Record: brand; category; capability; official surface and domain; direct URL; page title; path from site navigation; access date; desktop/mobile context; observed action and result; status; maturity score; novelty classification; short description; how it works; customer value; screenshot status and file or slide identifier; limitations; confidence. Capture the page state relevant to the claim; remove personal information. Cite source and date on every comparative claim. Prefer primary brand, distributor, dealer or service-provider pages, clearly labeled by ownership. Use third-party sources only as context, never proof that the brand site offers a capability.

## Comparison discipline

Apply identical definitions, navigation depth and inspection effort to every brand. Normalize scores only over applicable, inspected features; publish denominator and weighting if aggregating. Default to an unweighted category profile and avoid a single overall winner unless the user requests a ranking and the evidence coverage is adequate. Flag stale or conflicting pages, mobile-only/app-only functions, regional availability and login-gated claims. Differentiate “announced”, “visible”, “interactive” and “completed” stages. Recheck time-sensitive pricing and promotions before use.

## Novelty within the reviewed sample

Classify confirmed functionality using the sample denominator: Table Stakes = present in at least 75% of brands; Developing = present in 25% to under 75%; Distinctive = present in under 25%. Advanced describes independently evidenced functional depth or integration, regardless of prevalence, and may accompany any prevalence label. For small samples, state counts as well as percentages and avoid claims about the Indonesian market as a whole. Use “not confirmed” when evidence is insufficient. Do not treat promotional claims or unusual styling as innovation.

## Mandatory second-pass verification

Before publication, re-open the exact primary-source URL for each material cell and compare the claim to the observed input and output. Keep descriptions of an app or press announcement distinct from direct interaction. Recheck every score difference against the same definition for all brands. Revise the evidence register first, then regenerate the matrix, counts, top differences and conclusions. Use ? for uninspected or blocked functionality; reserve 0 for documented searches with no finding. Surface unresolved verification limits in the output.
