How the hub works

  • Coverage shows every brand and feature for the chosen country, and which have been checked.
  • Add and research is where you add brands, countries and features, ask Claude to research, and bring the answer back.
  • Results turns everything checked so far into the comparison dashboard, with the reasoning behind each score.

What the numbers mean

  • 0 Not found. We looked and it is not there.
  • 1 Information only, or a phone number or link elsewhere.
  • 2 A working tool on the site that gives a result.
  • 3 What the customer chose carries into a next step such as a dealer, finance or an order.
  • 4 The whole task can be finished online and tracked.
  • ? Could not be checked. N/A does not apply to this brand.

Working as a team

  • Everything is stored in one shared place. When a colleague saves results you see them without refreshing.
  • To add data you need Contributor access. Ask the person who owns this page to share it with you.
  • Rows marked with a dotted underline are low confidence. They are worth re-checking before a client sees them.
  • Each result records how it was checked, for example tested in a browser or navigation only.
